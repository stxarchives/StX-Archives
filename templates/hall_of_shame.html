Hall of Shame

Administrative failures that remain completely unaddressed.

Dead Computers

Only a few computers actually work. The lab is a graveyard of broken monitors, yet computer fees are mandatory.

Unpainted Walls

Corridors and classrooms look abandoned with scraped, unpainted walls, severely lacking basic maintenance.

Missing PT Teacher & Books

No physical education teacher has been appointed, and the mandatory Physical Education books are still entirely unavailable.

Broken Infrastructure

Washrooms are broken, drinking water is unsafe, benches are damaged, and almost every classroom has shattered windows.

Religious Discrimination

Students are forced to sing and dance to Christian songs. While holidays are given for Muslim and Christian festivals, Hindu festivals (like Hanuman Jayanti) are completely ignored.

Farewell Extortion & Junk Food

Extorting ₹1500+ for farewells (increasing by class), yet students must perform all the work while eating terrible food. Meanwhile, the canteen sells purely unhealthy junk food.
