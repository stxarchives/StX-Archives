Portal Guidelines

Verified Document

Initial guidelines for submitting and reviewing evidence on the transparency portal. These rules are designed to ensure that all information remains factual, objective, and neutral.

1. Factual Reporting

All submissions must be based on documented facts. Do not present disputed claims as established facts. Provide evidence such as photographs, official emails, or notices whenever possible.

2. Neutrality

The language used in submissions should be neutral and objective. Refrain from using inflammatory language or personal attacks.

3. Verification

Our moderation team will review all evidence before publication. Unverified accounts will be clearly marked as "Personal Experiences".
